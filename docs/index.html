Spicetify theme · free and open source

Spotify, made of glass and light.

Mirage turns Spotify into frosted glass. Behind it, the album cover drifts like a soft mist, and every song repaints the buttons, titles and progress bar in its own colours.

      Install

      One line, and you're done.

      Copy the line for your system, paste it into a terminal and press Enter. It installs Spicetify if needed and then Mirage.

      Open PowerShell (Start menu → type “PowerShell”) and paste:

      iwr -useb https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Passimoritz/spicetify-mirage/main/install.ps1 | iex

      Open the Terminal and paste:

      curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Passimoritz/spicetify-mirage/main/install.sh | bash
      • Installs Spicetify if it is missing (with its official installer)
      • Copies Mirage and switches it on
      • Safe to run again at any time, for example after a Spotify update

      You need the Spotify desktop app from spotify.com (on Windows not the Microsoft Store version).

      Already using Spicetify? Mirage is also in the Spicetify Marketplace under Themes.
